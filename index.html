Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wallspry (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Wallspry mobile application on Android (the “App”). By using Wallspry, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Wallspry is a wallpaper application that lets you browse, search, favorite, download, and set wallpapers on your device. Some features may require a paid Premium subscription. Free users may see advertisements.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following:

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and similar technical details.
  • Identifiers — advertising ID / device advertising identifiers, Firebase installation or anonymous auth identifiers, and similar IDs used for ads, analytics, crash reporting, and service delivery.
  • Usage and diagnostics — screens viewed, feature interactions (for example downloads, favorites, search), performance data, and crash logs.
  • Subscription status — whether Premium is active, restored, or expired (payment card details are handled by Google Play, not by us).
  • Local app data — favorites, download history metadata, theme and notification preferences stored on your device.
  • Notifications — if you enable notifications, we may use a push token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver updates.

We do not require you to create a Google account inside Wallspry. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location for account sign-up.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve Wallspry (browse, search, download, favorites, set wallpaper, Premium unlock).
  • Show personalized or non-personalized ads to free users through Google AdMob.
  • Measure app performance and stability with analytics and crash reporting.
  • Verify and manage Premium subscriptions purchased through Google Play.
  • Send optional push notifications you allow.
  • Prevent fraud/abuse, secure the App, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you are not subscribed to Premium, Wallspry may display banner, interstitial, and/or rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect or receive device identifiers, IP address, and usage signals to provide and measure ads, and (where permitted) to personalize ads.

Learn more in Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy
  • Google Ad Settings

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android device settings. Premium removes ads while the subscription remains active.

5. Analytics, crash reporting, and messaging

Wallspry uses Google Firebase services, which may process device and usage data:

  • Firebase Analytics — aggregated usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability reports.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications (if enabled).
  • Firebase Authentication / Firestore / Remote Config / Storage — anonymous service identifiers, content configuration, and related backend features.

See the Firebase Privacy and Security page for details.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive purchase/restore confirmation and subscription status so we can unlock Premium features. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

7. Data stored on your device

Favorites, downloads metadata, theme mode, notification preferences, and similar settings may be stored locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Wallspry may remove this data from your device.

8. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • With service providers such as Google (AdMob, Firebase, Play Billing) that process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their policies.
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • In connection with a business transfer (for example merger or asset sale), where permitted by law.

We do not sell your personal information as a consumer data broker.

9. Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Analytics and crash data retention follows the defaults of the third-party tools we use. Local data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App.

10. International transfers

Our service providers (including Google) may process data on servers located outside your country. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards described in their privacy documentation.

11. Children’s privacy

Wallspry is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can:

  • Clear cache and related local data in App Settings (where available).
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data.
  • Upgrade to Premium to remove ads.
  • Manage notifications in App Settings and system settings.
  • Manage advertising preferences via device and Google Ad Settings.
  • Contact us using the email below to make a privacy request.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Wallspry after an update means you accept the revised policy.
